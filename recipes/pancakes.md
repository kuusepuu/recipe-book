# Fluffy Pancakes

Thick, soft breakfast pancakes that are ready in 20 minutes.

- **Servings:** 4 (about 10 pancakes)
- **Prep time:** 5 min
- **Cook time:** 15 min

## Ingredients

- 200 g all-purpose flour
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 pinch of salt
- 250 ml milk
- 1 egg
- 2 tbsp melted butter, plus a little more for the pan

## Instructions

1. Whisk the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt together in a large bowl.
2. In a second bowl, whisk the milk, egg and melted butter.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ones and stir just until combined. A few lumps are fine.
4. Let the batter rest for 5 minutes.
5. Heat a frying pan over medium heat and brush it with butter.
6. Pour about 60 ml (¼ cup) of batter per pancake.
7. Cook until bubbles appear on top and the edges look set (about 2 minutes), then flip and cook 1 more minute.
8. Serve warm with berries, jam or maple syrup.

## Tips

- Don't overmix the batter, or the pancakes come out tough.
- Keep cooked pancakes warm in a 90 °C oven while you make the rest.
